# Sample Q&A — ACU Chatbot Test Results
## Week 4 Testing — With Context Injection

### Q1: What faculties does Acibadem University have?
Acibadem University has six faculties which include the Faculty of Medicine, Faculty of Dentistry, Faculty of Pharmacy, Faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences with programs such as Computer Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Genetics and Bioengineering. It also includes the Faculty of Health Sciences and the Faculty of Economics and Administrative Sciences.

### Q2: Where is Acibadem University located?
Acibadem University is located in Istanbul, Turkey, with main campuses in Altunizade and Maslak districts.

### Q3: How can I apply to Acibadem University?
To apply to Acibadem University, you can do so through the Turkish university entrance exam (YKS) if you are a domestic student. If you're an international student, applications should be submitted directly via the ACU website at https://acibadem.edu.tr/.
